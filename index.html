About Easter

The Meaning of Easter

Easter is a festive spring holiday celebrating themes of rebirth, renewal, and new beginnings. Originally a religious holiday, it has blossomed into a global celebration of love, family, and joy.

Traditions abound during this season. From colorful Easter eggs symbolizing new life to church celebrations and joyous family gatherings, Easter is a time of happiness and gratitude.
